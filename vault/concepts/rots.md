# ROTS (Realistic Opportunity to See)

**ROTS** is a frame-level metric: the part of Total Traffic consisting only of people who pass within the visible area of a frame going (or looking) in a direction such that the display of the frame can be seen. [^1]

Formula: `ROTS = Total Traffic × Facility Traffic Share` [^2]

ROTS feeds directly into [VAC](vac.md) and [Viewable Impressions](impression.md).
