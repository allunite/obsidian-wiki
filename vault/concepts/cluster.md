# Cluster

A **cluster** is a logical grouping of facilities associated with a venue such as a shopping mall, retail park, airport, or transport hub. Cluster membership is defined by whether the majority of a facility's traffic is generated by visitors to the venue (not general street passersby). [^1]

## Membership Criteria

**Belongs to cluster if:**
- Located on premises (exterior walls, parking, entrances).
- Majority of traffic comes from venue visitors.

**Does not belong if:**
- On the outer side of a building facing a public street.
- Majority of traffic is general street flow. [^1]

## Categories and Analytics Modules

Each cluster must be assigned a category. The cluster's category determines which analytics module it appears in:

| Analytics Module | Categories |
|-----------------|------------|
| Mall Analytics | Mall, Supermarket |
| Park Analytics | Park, Golf Club, Ski_Resort, Golf_Resort, Arena |
| Transit Analytics | Taxi Rank, Transport Hub, Rail, Metro, Ferry, Train, Gas_Station, Underground, Parking Hall |
| Airport Analytics | Airport |

Category names are case-sensitive and prefix-matched (e.g., "Mall_240" matches "Mall"). [^2]

## Linking

For facilities without sensors, linking rules determine which sensor-equipped facility provides traffic data. See [linking-rules.md](linking-rules.md). [^3]
