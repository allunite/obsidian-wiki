# Insertion

An **insertion** is one advertisement shown on a screen. It can be a static image or a video ad. [^1]

**Also known as:** Spot, Play, Ad Slot (depending on platform or media buyer).

Each insertion has a **length** (insert duration) — how long it is shown on screen. For non-digital (printed) screens, only one insertion is displayed at a time. For digital or rotated facilities, multiple insertions play in a repeating sequence called a [loop](loop.md).

Each time an insertion appears on a [frame](frame.md), it is counted as one insertion.
