## Summary

Footfall discrepancy discovered for Singapore client JCD (company 7978). Platform reported significantly different daily footfall totals depending on aggregation method used, caused by a bug in the hourly `clusterFrequency` calculation.

---

## Technical Detail

**Affected table:** `device_session_agg_5`  
**Affected metric:** Hourly cluster-level footfall / `clusterFrequency`

**Root cause:** When `session_number` (frequency period) equals 0 but `session_number_predicted` is non-zero, the hourly footfall calculation produced incorrect results. The bug specifically affected the `clusterFrequency` value used in aggregation.

**Observed discrepancy (2025-12-09):**
- Method A: 125,948
- Method B: 45,798
- Platform reported value: 52,406

**Affected sites:** Raffles Mall and ION Orchard (Singapore).

---

## Resolution

Fixed in Release 10 (v2026.04.14). Task closed 2026-03-09.
