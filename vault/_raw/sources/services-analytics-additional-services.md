**Raw path:** `raw/docs.allunite.com/Services/Analytics additional services.md`

## Summary

Two supplementary analytics services: **Management KPI Dashboard** at `kpi.allunite.com` — built on Apache Superset, shows aggregated data from the Analytics platform. **PostHog** at `posthog.com` — used to track analytics platform usage (product analytics).
