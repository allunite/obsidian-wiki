**Raw path:** `raw/docs.allunite.com/Operations/Facility WiKi/Frame types.md`

## Summary

Defines 13 frame type codes used in the inventory system: OTD (outdoor), MNT (monument), TPP (totem/pylon), MUP (mid-size urban panel), WAL (wall), CLK (clock), WRP (wraparound), BAN (banner), TWR (tower), TRN (transport), NSK (newsstand/kiosk), BSH (bus shelter), OTH (other).

See [frame-types.md](../concepts/frame-types.md).
