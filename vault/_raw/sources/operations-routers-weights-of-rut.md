## Summary

Physical weight specifications for Teltonika hardware used in sensor installations: RUT200 Router (125 g/unit), EU PSU (94 g/unit), SISO Combo Antenna (215 ± 10 g/unit). All follow standard mass-packaging of 24 units per box. Supplier: Teltonika Networks.
