# Source: Clockify (Generic)

**Raw path:** `raw/docs.allunite.com/Generic/Clockify.md`

## Summary

Company-wide Clockify time tracking policy. Five task categories: **PreSales** (pre-contract), **New Project** (post-contract to delivery), **Maintains** (post-delivery support), **R&D** (research), **Internal** (non-client tasks), **ClickUp** (time spent updating ClickUp statuses). Rules: one project = one company; always include client name; weekly timesheet due by Friday EOD; Data/Ops internal time cap: 15 hours/month. Regional meetings logged under country/region project (Brazil/LATAM, ASIA, EUROPE, AFRICA). Internal meetings under "Internal OT (DT or IT)"; mixed meetings can be split.
