# Source: Terminology (Ukrainian OOH Glossary)

**Raw path:** `raw/docs.allunite.com/Operations/Terminology.md`

## Summary

Ukrainian-language glossary of OOH/DOOH industry terminology. Key sections:

**Audience Metrics:** Reach (Охоплення), Frequency (Частота), Impacts (Контакти = Reach × Frequency), GRP (Gross Rating Points = Impacts as % of market population), CPM/CPT (Cost per Thousand).

**Visibility Levels:** Circulation (all passersby), OTS (in visibility zone), OTC/ROTS (in zone and facing screen), VAC (high probability of actually seeing; gold standard), LTS (Likelihood to See = synonym for VAC), Viewability.

**DOOH & Programmatic:** DOOH, Spot, Impression Multiplier, Dwell Time, Programmatic, SSP, DSP.

**Data & Tech:** Traffic Intensity Model (TIM), Travel Survey, SDK Data, Bid Stream Data, MST (Multi-Sensor Tracking), Watcher (Quividi term for face recognized looking at screen).

**Governance:** JIC (Joint Industry Committee), Inventory, Currency.
