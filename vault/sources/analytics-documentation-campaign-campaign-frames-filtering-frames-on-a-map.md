# Source: Filtering Frames on a Map

**Raw path:** `raw/docs.allunite.com/Analytics Documentation/Campaign/Campaign Frames/Filtering frames on a map.md`

## Summary

UI feature allowing users to draw a polygon on the campaign map to filter frames geographically. Toggle "Select Frames on the map" to ON, click the polygon icon, draw the area, and only frames inside will be selected. Toggle OFF to remove the filter.

See [campaign.md](../concepts/campaign.md).
