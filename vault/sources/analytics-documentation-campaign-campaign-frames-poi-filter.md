# Source: POI Filter

**Raw path:** `raw/docs.allunite.com/Analytics Documentation/Campaign/Campaign Frames/POI filter.md`

## Summary

Frames can be filtered by proximity to Points of Interest (POIs): include frames within N meters of a POI category, and/or exclude frames within M meters of another category (e.g., include near supermarkets within 300m, exclude near schools within 400m for alcohol advertising). POI data is sourced from Google; clients can upload their own POI files.

See [campaign.md](../concepts/campaign.md).
