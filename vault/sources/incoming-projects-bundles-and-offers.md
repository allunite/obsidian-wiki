# Source: Bundles and Offers Proposal

**Raw path:** `raw/docs.allunite.com/Incoming Projects/Bundles and Offers - Proposal.md`

## Summary

Proposal (January 2026) to replace the "Package" concept with two distinct concepts in MOPS:

**Bundle:** Fixed group of frames sold together (all-or-nothing). Created by media owner. CPM = sum of frame CPMs. Versioned (new version when frames added/removed; campaigns retain their original version).

**Offer:** Commercial product with availability period. Contains frames and/or bundles. CPM = calculated or custom override. Lifecycle: Draft → Active → Inactive/Expired. Created by sales team. Versioned.

**Campaign inventory types:** Individual frames (free add/remove), Bundles (all-or-nothing), Offers (all-or-nothing). Duplicate frames across sources prevented by system.

**Deprecation of Combined Campaigns:** To be replaced by Bundles (frame grouping) and Offers (commercial packaging). Existing Combined Campaigns to be migrated.
