# Source: Data/Ops Team Operating Model

**Raw path:** `raw/docs.allunite.com/Operations/Data%2FOps Team Operating Model.md`

## Summary

8-section document defining how the Data/Ops team operates. Sections: (1) Task ownership — one owner per task, clear accountability; (2) Scope control — define scope before starting, escalate changes; (3) Budget escalation — flag when work exceeds estimate; (4) Blockers — report blockers immediately, don't sit on them; (5) Clockify tracking — all billable work tracked; (6) Client communications — route through designated channel; (7) R&D — separate tracking from delivery work; (8) Team rhythm — regular syncs and async updates.

See [operations.md](../operations.md).
